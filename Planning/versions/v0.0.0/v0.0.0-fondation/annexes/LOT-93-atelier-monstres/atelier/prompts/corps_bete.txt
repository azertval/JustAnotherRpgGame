The creature fights with its own body, named in the WEAPON line: no weapon, no armour,
no saddle, no collar, nothing held. It always faces the viewer's right, where it
strikes: the head is on the right of the figure, the tail on the left, in every frame
of every row. A strike may leave a short trail of claw or fang marks that starts at the
claws or the jaws and stays inside the cell; nothing else glows or sparkles.
