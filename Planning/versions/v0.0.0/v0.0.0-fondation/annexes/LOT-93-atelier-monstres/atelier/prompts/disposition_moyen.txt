LAYOUT — ONE SPRITE SHEET: create ONE pixel-art sprite sheet, a single 1536x2048
portrait image with a transparent background, holding ALL the animations of ONE
creature, organised as an invisible grid of 8 rows, each 256 px tall. One animation
(or one half of an animation) per row, one frame per cell, frames read left to right,
unused cells left empty. Each row contains EXACTLY the number of separate figures
given below, never more, never fewer, with clearly visible empty space (at least
16 px) between two neighbouring figures and their effects; nothing touches or crosses
into a neighbouring cell. In every frame the feet rest on the same baseline, 40 px
above the bottom of the cell.

Reference image 1 is the finished sprite sheet of ANOTHER character, laid out exactly
like this one: copy its grid, pixel size, outline and shading, and the kind of motion
in each row; never its face, body, outfit, weapon, effects or colours.

It is the same creature, drawn at the same size, in every frame of every row: same
head, body, markings, equipment and colours from row 1 to row 8.

{CORPS}

ROWS 1 TO 4 — cells 192 px wide (row 4: 256 px wide), feet centred in the cell:
Row 1 - IDLE, exactly 6 frames, six separate figures: 1 guard, 2 breathe in, 3 chest highest, 4 breathe out, 5 chest lowest, 6 almost frame 1 so it loops.
Row 2 - {MARCHE}
Row 3 - HIT, exactly 4 frames, four separate figures in the first four cells, never three: 1 FLINCH, the blow lands, the head jerks back; 2 RECOIL, leaning furthest back, one foot slides back; 3 RECOVER, straightening up; 4 GUARD, back to the idle pose of row 1.
Row 4 - DEATH, exactly 6 frames: stagger (1-3), fall (4), lying on the ground (5-6), still on the baseline. The lying body is centred on the spot where the feet stood and, tail, cloak and weapon included, is no longer than twice the standing height.

ROWS 5 TO 8 — cells 384 px wide, exactly 4 frames per row. Stand the creature with
its feet one third of the way across the cell from the left, so that strikes and
effects have room on the right: an effect reaches at most 1.2 times the creature's
height to the right of the feet (it must end inside the cell, well before its right
edge), and nothing reaches further left than 0.6 times the creature's height.
Row 5 - ATTACK frames 1-4: wind-up (1-3), start of the strike (4).
Row 6 - ATTACK frames 5-8: end of the strike (5), recovery (6-8); frame 8 is almost the idle pose of row 1.
