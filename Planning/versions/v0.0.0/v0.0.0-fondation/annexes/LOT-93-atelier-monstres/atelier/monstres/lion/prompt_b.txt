CREATURE: a lion, the arena beast of the Imperial Coliseum (Large beast, no spells).
LOOK: a big male lion, tawny coat, a thick dark-brown mane that frames the head and covers the neck and chest, a pale muzzle, amber eyes, a long tail ending in a dark tuft; lean and muscular, a few old pale scars on the flanks from past fights.
SILHOUETTE: the mane makes the head twice as wide as the neck; a long low body on four heavy legs; the tail curls up behind. At its small size the shape must read as a big cat with a mane, never as a dog.
SIZE: LARGE, it fills a square of 2 x 2 tiles. Seen in three-quarter view from the front, slightly from above, facing the viewer's right: about 88 px tall from the paws to the top of the mane, about 168 px long from the nose to the rump, tail curled so it adds little length.
WEAPON: claws and bite; the strike is a lunge to the right, forepaw raised to rake, jaws open.
PALETTE (taken from reference image 3; use only these plus their darker/lighter tones): #5B3322, #39241F, #99602F, #CF9550, #754426, #C2843D, #EACA96.
ANIMATION SET: rows 1 to 6; rows 7 and 8 empty.
