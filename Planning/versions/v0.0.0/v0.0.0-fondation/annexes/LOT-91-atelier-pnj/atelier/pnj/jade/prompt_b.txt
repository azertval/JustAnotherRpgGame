CHARACTER: Jade, the Bard - a young human bard (Medium humanoid).
LOOK (match reference images 3, 4 and 5): long dark hair with violet streaks, a small jewel on
the forehead, gold choker and arm rings; loose white short-sleeved top, long crimson skirt,
bare arms; no armour.
WEAPON: a carved wooden lute held across the body; she strikes with it swung like a club in
ATTACK; green-teal magic notes may swirl around it.
PALETTE (taken from reference image 5; use only these plus their darker/lighter tones): #4B2A31, #EC9D62, #D2784E, #9B5431, #9D68A3, #F7DBB7, #B9988A; accent colours (weapon, magic, effects): #0FAD87, #95F09F, #68DD95.
ANIMATION SET: all eight rows. CAST is a bard song, not a hand spell: both hands play the lute;
row 7 (frames 1-4) she strums, swaying, and green-teal magic notes appear and swirl around the lute;
row 8 (frames 5-8) a final strum sends a short wave of notes toward the right that stays inside the cell (5-6),
then she returns to her idle guard with the lute (7-8).
