STYLE (match reference image 1, the approved scene, exactly): detailed 16-bit
pixel art for an isometric RPG, {PAS} screen pixels per art pixel, crisp square
pixels, no anti-aliasing, no blur, no photographic texture. Dark outline one
pixel wide in a deep warm brown or navy, never pure black. Three to five tones
per material, hand-placed dithering allowed, no smooth gradients.

VIEW: fixed isometric camera, no perspective. Floor tiles are flat diamonds
{LOSANGE_L} screen pixels wide and {LOSANGE_H} tall. Walls and objects stand on the diamond

top-left, and warm pools of lantern light on the ground and walls around each
lantern, drawn as flat stepped tones (no bloom, no lens glow).
